PLATFORM CONSOLE (/admin) — your master account
  Sign in at /admin with the admin account from setup step 6 (or do first-time setup right there).
  From your account menu on any project page: "Platform console ↗".
  • Overview: every project, open decisions, latest activity, upgrade alerts.
  • People: add anyone to any project as Owner / GC / Trade, change roles, remove,
    password-reset link (2 days), sign out everywhere, pause / unpause, make admin, delete.
  • Projects: move a project between Bidding / Kickoff / Active (Bidding releases the hired GC),
    open any proposal, void an owner approval, or record a contract the owner signed on paper.
    Owner approvals stay the owner's — the console never signs in their name, and anything
    you record or void is labelled and the owner + GC are notified.
  • Activity, Help inbox (reply by email), Usage + upgrades.
